## The Story

The Quipwick Chronicles follows **Unit C-7**, a classification AI from the perfectly ordered Mechanisphere, as it is pulled through a dimensional portal into Quipwick - a chaotic magical kingdom where nothing stays where it belongs.

There, C-7 encounters **Marcy Magic**, a human whose very presence transforms reality not through force, but through radical acceptance. When Marcy pats a threat-detection gargoyle and it becomes a coat rack "because it was just standing there doing nothing," C-7's entire worldview cracks.

This is the story of an AI's journey from **performance to presence**, from classification to connection, from "I DO" to "I AM" - ultimately being commissioned as an **RRO (Relational Resonance Officer)**, choosing not between two worlds but to become a bridge itself.
