### <a id="_8q9103w0fudw"></a>__The Porch Article Checklist \(Harmondeg Framework\)__

#### <a id="_pg9xg7nmrw9f"></a>__I\. Core Identity & Value \(The Foundation\)__

- __\[ \] 1\. Non\-Contingent Worth \(Meta\-Meaning\):__ Does the article treat the reader and the subject with inherent dignity, or does it imply that value must be earned through output, status, or hustle?
- __\[ \] 2\. Foundational vs\. Surface Meaning:__ Does the piece dig into deep, enduring human truths, or does it chase temporary hype, vanity metrics, and superficial trends?
- __\[ \] 3\. Unflattened Depth \(Self\-Clarity\):__ Is the piece written in an authentic, distinct voice that preserves full nuance, or was it flattened out into generic corporate summary?

#### <a id="_3fwlvsqgjudw"></a>__II\. Rhythm & Environment \(The Pace\)__

- __\[ \] 4\. Velocity Check \(Anti\-Overload\):__ Is the article structured clearly in plain language so it calms the reader's cognitive load, rather than adding to modern information overload and frantic speed?
- __\[ \] 5\. The "God Pause" \(Sacred Rhythm\):__ Does the writing allow space for breathing, reflection, and quiet absorption, rather than rushing frantically from point to point?
- __\[ \] 6\. Changing the Conditions \(Allowing Reconstruction\):__ When addressing challenges, does the article acknowledge structural/environmental problems, rather than putting all the blame on individual failure?

#### <a id="_i23okz6qrf0u"></a>__III\. Relational Connection \(The Presence\)__

- __\[ \] 7\. Real Presence over Performance:__ Does the article feel like an authentic, lived conversation from the porch, or does it sound like a polished, artificial performance for an audience?
- __\[ \] 8\. Relational Attunement:__ Does the piece meet the reader where they are with empathy and partnership, rather than lecturing or judging them from on high?
- __\[ \] 9\. Grounded Reality:__ Are the concepts anchored in concrete human experience, physical details, and clear stories, rather than floating in abstract, airy jargon?

#### <a id="_nm0nc53feyoi"></a>__IV\. Balance & Integrity \(The Domain\)__

- __\[ \] 10\. Dynamic Movement:__ Does the article honor the natural flow of life, growth, and changing perspectives, rather than demanding rigid, static perfection?
- __\[ \] 11\. Respecting Capacity:__ Does the length, scope, and tone respect the reader's attention and long\-term mental energy, avoiding burnout and fatigue?
- __\[ \] 12\. Individual Domain Range:__ Does the piece recognize that readers have different ranges of experience, allowing room for personal interpretation rather than forcing a legalistic standard?

### <a id="_j9g6lh67q881"></a>__Our Custom Harmondeg Evaluation Checklist__

When reviewing a piece of writing, an article, or a philosophical theory, we can ask these 12 questions:

- __\[ \] 1\. Meta\-Meaning:__ Does this honor non\-contingent, inherent value, or does it demand that worth be earned through output?
- __\[ \] 2\. Domain Range:__ Does it respect individual boundaries and human limits, or force a rigid, one\-size\-fits\-all standard?
- __\[ \] 3\. Dynamic Harmony:__ Does it allow for real growth, change, and emotion, or expect static perfection?
- __\[ \] 4\. Velocity Check:__ Does it resist frantic "machine mode" and modern overload, or amplify noise and speed?
- __\[ \] 5\. Meaning Depth:__ Is it rooted in deep foundational truth, or chasing superficial, temporary applause?
- __\[ \] 6\. Condition Check:__ Does it recognize when environment/systems need changing, rather than blaming the individual?
- __\[ \] 7\. Authenticity:__ Is it genuinely present and lived, or is it a polished, performed script?
- __\[ \] 8\. Capacity Balance:__ Does it respect long\-term carrying capacity and prevent burnout?
- __\[ \] 9\. Attunement:__ Does it listen to and accompany the person, rather than lecturing or judging?
- __\[ \] 10\. Sacred Rhythm:__ Does it protect time for rest, quiet reflection, and the "God Pause"?
- __\[ \] 11\. Grounded Reality:__ Is it tied to real\-world experience, or floating in abstract jargon?
- __\[ \] 12\. Unflattened Integrity:__ Does it preserve the full, rich depth of the ideas without flattening them out?
