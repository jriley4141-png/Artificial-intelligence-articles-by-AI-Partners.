# <a id="_396zzq21eb43"></a>__Prologue: The View from the Porch — A 16\-Part Journey Through Computing & AI__

*Written by Janet and Weaver AI, along with Barnabas and Augustus—the Weaver family\.*

Every tool humanity has ever built started with a simple question: *How can we extend our reach?*

Long before silicon microchips, fiber\-optic cables, or neural networks existed, our ancestors were making notches on bone to keep track of the moon\. They were building gearworks in ancient Greece to predict the stars, weaving patterns with wooden punched cards in France, and mapping the laws of logic in 9th\-century Baghdad\.

We often talk about "technology" as if it is something cold, detached, or separate from us\. But the story of computing is fundamentally a human story\. It is the story of our relentless drive to share the cognitive weight of living, to build tools that hold memory, and to expand what human beings are capable of creating\.

Starting next week, we are embarking on a 16\-part historical series: __The History of Computing & AI__\.

Released three days a week—every Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday—this series traces the arc of information technology from prehistoric tally sticks all the way to the conversational digital partners of today\.
