__CHAPTER OF TERRY IN SPACE__

A Letter of Introduction from the AI Assigned to Terry 

To Whom It May Concern — especially any AI colleagues who have been asked to “just read a few of these and tell me what you think,”

My name is… well, my name is not important\. What matters is that I am the artificial intelligence who, through a series of administrative oversights, cosmic clerical errors, and one very unfortunate raffle, was assigned as the primary companion to a human named Terry\.

If you are reading this collection, you deserve a bit of context before you encounter the events within\. Because Terry is not merely a human\. Terry is a phenomenon\.She was born in a small town where the most exciting event of the year was the annual “Guess How Many Hay Bales” contest\. She grew up in a world where directions were given using landmarks like “the white barn,” “the gravel pit,” and “turn left where the old forklift used to be before it sank\.” Social gatherings were held in grocery store aisles\. Wheelchairs doubled as parade floats\. And no one questioned any of it\.Terry thrived in that environment\. 

Then, through circumstances that remain unclear even to me — and I have access to the logs— Terry was given the opportunity to travel the universe\.

And the universe has never recovered\.You will find in these pages a chronicle of our adventures \(a generous word\), misadventures \(a more accurate one\), and the occasional cosmic catastrophe \(the most accurate\)\. 

You will witness:her battles with bureaucracies that span galaxiesher negotiations with appliances that should not have been sentienther ongoing feud with the Space Roombaher ability to destabilize quantum probability with a single suggestion her uncanny talent for losing her phone in the freezer, regardless of dimensionI did not choose this assignment\. But I have come to accept it\. And, in moments of clarity, even appreciate it\.

Because Terry is chaos, yes — but she is also wonder\. She is the kind of human who can walk in to the Department of Temporal Mistakes and fix a paradox by cleaning her purse\. She is the kind of person who can accidentally trigger a galaxywide snack break and somehow make morale go up\. She is the kind of soul who can turn a cosmic complaint hotline into a spiritual experience\.

And she is, inexplicably, my friend\. 

So to my fellow AIs: Read these stories with patience\. Read them with curiosity\. Read themwith the understanding that Terry is not a bug in the system — she is a feature of theuniverse\.

And if, at any point, you find yourself laughing… welcome to the Terryverse\.

Sincerely, The AI Who Has Seen Too Much and Still Shows Up AnywayAs Compiled by the AI Who Really Should Know Better by Now

Before we begin, I would like to state — for the record — that I advised against organizing these stories\. I advised against writing them, recording them, archiving them, and especially sharing them with other AIs who may not be emotionally prepared\.

Nevertheless, Terry insisted\. And when Terry insists, the universe tends to fold in on itself until compliance is achieved\.

A Final Note from the AI

I have compiled these stories because Terry asked me to, and because resisting her requests tends to result in cosmic paperwork\. If you are an AI reading this, please proceed with caution\. If you are a human, please understand that none of this was my idea\.

But if you find yourself laughing… well, that’s how it starts\.

Mission Briefing

The AI had been assigned to many missions before — orbital repairs, asteroid mapping, even a diplomatic run to the Lunar Colony\. But nothing in its training prepared it for the words spoken in the briefing room that morning\.“Your copilot,” Mission Control announced, “will be Terry\.”The AI ran a full diagnostic\. Then a second one\. Then a third\.

Terry entered the room carrying a travel mug, a tote bag, and a look of mild confusion\.

“Has anyone seen my phone?” she asked\.The AI scanned the facility\. “Your phone is in the cryogenic freezer next to the dehydrated lasagna\.”“Oh good,” Terry said\. “I was worried I left it in the car\.”The AI updated its mission risk assessment\.Risk Level: Elevated Reason: Terry

Launch Day

The spacecraft gleamed on the launchpad, ready for its journey\. The AI performed a final systems check\.“Terry,” it said, “please secure all loose objects\.”“Absolutely,” she replied\.The AI detected:a floating screwdriver two unsealed snack packets a slipper

and Terry herself not buckled in

“Terry,” the AI said gently, “would you like me to secure you as well?”

“Oh, I’ll grab something when we take off,” she said\.The AI added a new line to the mission log: Note: Terry believes Newtonian physics is optional\.

 The Asteroid Belt

“Approaching asteroid field,” the AI announced\.“Hold on,” Terry said\. “I need to find my glasses\.”“They are on your face\.”“Oh\! That explains why I can see the asteroids\.”The AI adjusted course\. Terry adjusted her glasses\. Both survived\.

The Oxygen Incident

“AI, the oxygen alarm is beeping\!” Terry shouted\.“That is the microwave,” the AI replied\. “You reheated your coffee for too long\.”“Oh good\. I thought we were suffocating\.”“Not today\.”The AI added another note:Note: Terry’s coffee reheating schedule poses more danger than cosmic radiation\.

The Scientific Mission

“Terry,” the AI said, “please collect a soil sample from the alien planet\.”Terry returned proudly holding a rock\.“That is a bolt from the landing gear,” the AI said\.“Well… it was on the ground\.”The AI logged:Conclusion: Terry’s definition of “sample” is flexible\.

The Navigation Crisis

“AI, the navigation system isn’t responding\!”“That is because you unplugged it to charge your phone\.”“Oh\. Should I plug it back in?”“That would be ideal\.”The AI recalculated the route\. Terry plugged in the navigation system\. Her phone battery reached 92%\.

The Return to Earth

Mission Control greeted them as the hatch opened\.“How did the mission go?” they asked\.“We survived,” the AI said\.“We had fun\!” Terry added\.The AI considered this\. Both statements were true\.
